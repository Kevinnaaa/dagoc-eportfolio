My Journey in Taking the Professional Education Course

A Journey of Discovery, Growth, and New Beginnings

by Brendan Jadrian W. Dagoc

Introduction: The Beginning of a New Journey

Life is a continuous journey of learning, discovering new opportunities, and making decisions that can shape our future. Sometimes, we begin with one dream, but as we gain experience and understand ourselves better, we realize that there are other paths worth pursuing. This is how I describe my journey toward enrolling in the Professional Education Course (PEC). As a graduate of Bachelor of Science in Hotel and Restaurant Management (BSRM), I never imagined that I would eventually pursue another course related to education. However, as time passed and I gained more experience in my professional life, I began to recognize the importance of expanding my knowledge, improving my qualifications, and preparing myself for other career opportunities.

Enrolling in the PEC course was not an easy decision because it meant accepting another responsibility while already working and managing other aspects of my life. It required me to step outside my comfort zone and become a student once again. I had to prepare myself for new lessons, academic requirements, group activities, classroom discussions, and interactions with people whom I had never met before. Despite these challenges, I decided to take this opportunity because I believed that investing in education is one of the best ways to improve myself and prepare for a better future.

For me, PEC is more than just a second course. It represents my willingness to learn something new, explore another profession, and open doors to opportunities that I may not have considered before. It is also a reminder that our educational journey does not necessarily end when we receive our first diploma. As long as we are willing to learn and improve, there will always be opportunities to discover new possibilities and pursue our dreams.

My Background as a Graduate and a Government Employee

After completing my bachelor's degree in Hotel and Restaurant Management, I began building my professional experience and exploring opportunities related to my education. My degree provided me with knowledge and skills in hospitality, customer service, communication, teamwork, and dealing with different types of people. These competencies helped shape my professional attitude and taught me the importance of responsibility, patience, and dedication in the workplace.

At present, I am working as a contractual employee at JH Cerilles State College (JHCSC), Pagadian Annex. Being part of a government institution has allowed me to experience the importance of public service, professionalism, accountability, and cooperation. Through my work, I have learned that every employee has a role in contributing to the goals of an institution. Regardless of our position, our responsibilities matter because they help ensure that daily operations run smoothly and that the people we serve receive the assistance they need.

However, my present employment has also encouraged me to think about my long-term career plans. As a contractual employee, I understand the importance of preparing myself for future opportunities and finding ways to strengthen my professional qualifications. I want to continue growing in my current field while also exploring other possibilities that may provide greater career stability and personal fulfillment.

This is one of the reasons I decided to pursue the Professional Education Course. I realized that completing the necessary professional education requirements could help prepare me for a future teaching career. I also hope that having additional qualifications will broaden my opportunities when applying for appropriate government positions. Although I understand that regular employment depends on the available position, required qualifications, and applicable selection process, I believe that preparing myself through education is a meaningful step toward my goals.

My experience as a government employee has taught me that career development requires initiative. We cannot always wait for opportunities to come to us; sometimes, we need to prepare ourselves so that when an opportunity becomes available, we are ready to take it. Through PEC, I am investing in my personal and professional development and giving myself the chance to explore a different direction in life.

Discovering My Desire to Become a Teacher

One of the most important realizations I gained before enrolling in PEC was my desire to become a licensed professional teacher. As a graduate of a hospitality-related program, I realized that my knowledge and experiences could be useful not only in the workplace but also in educating and guiding future students. The hospitality and tourism industry requires individuals who possess practical skills, professionalism, good communication, and a strong sense of responsibility. I believe that these are valuable lessons that can be shared with students who aspire to pursue similar careers.

This realization made me understand that teaching could become another meaningful profession for me. I began to see the possibility of standing in front of a classroom, sharing what I have learned, guiding students through their challenges, and helping them prepare for their future careers. The thought of contributing to another person's learning and development gave me a new perspective on my professional goals.

At the same time, I understand that having knowledge of a particular field does not automatically make someone an effective teacher. Teaching requires specific knowledge, skills, and attitudes that must be developed through professional preparation. A teacher must understand how students learn, how to prepare appropriate lessons, how to assess learning, and how to manage a classroom with different personalities and learning needs. This is why I recognized the importance of taking PEC.

Through this course, I am beginning to understand the difference between knowing something and knowing how to teach it effectively. A person may be knowledgeable in a particular subject, but an effective teacher must also be able to explain concepts clearly, provide meaningful learning experiences, encourage participation, and help students overcome difficulties. This realization has made me appreciate the value of professional education and the preparation required to become a responsible and competent educator.

My dream of becoming a licensed teacher is still a work in progress, but I am willing to take the necessary steps to achieve it. I know that there will be more requirements, challenges, and responsibilities ahead. Nevertheless, I believe that every lesson I learn in PEC brings me closer to understanding the teaching profession and preparing myself for the possibility of becoming part of it.

My First Experiences as a PEC Student

Returning to the classroom as a student has given me a different perspective on education. Since I already completed my bachelor's degree and am currently working, I entered this course with expectations, responsibilities, and experiences that influenced the way I approached learning. However, I quickly realized that being a student again requires humility and an open mind. Regardless of our age, educational background, or work experience, there is always something new to learn.

During my PEC journey, I encountered lessons and activities that challenged me to think critically, express my ideas, participate in discussions, and work with others. There were moments when I needed to adjust to the requirements of the course and understand concepts that were unfamiliar to me. These experiences reminded me that learning is not always easy, but it becomes meaningful when we are willing to make an effort and learn from our mistakes.

I also realized that being a PEC student is not only about completing assignments and passing examinations. It is about developing the mindset and qualities needed in the teaching profession. Every classroom discussion, written reflection, group presentation, and learning activity provides an opportunity to practice communication, critical thinking, collaboration, and problem-solving.

Through these experiences, I have become more aware of my strengths and weaknesses. I learned that I need to continue improving my confidence, communication skills, patience, and ability to express my thoughts clearly. At the same time, I recognized that my previous educational and professional experiences are strengths that I can bring into my new learning environment.

Most importantly, I learned to accept that being a learner means being willing to make mistakes and improve. I do not need to know everything immediately. What matters is my willingness to listen, ask questions, participate, and apply the lessons I gain throughout the course.

The Importance of Meeting New Friends and Classmates

One of the most enjoyable parts of my PEC journey has been meeting new friends and classmates. Entering a new learning environment can be challenging because we begin among people whose backgrounds, personalities, experiences, and goals may be different from our own. At first, interacting with unfamiliar people may require effort, but as we participate in class activities and communicate with one another, we gradually develop friendships and build a sense of belonging.

Meeting my classmates has allowed me to understand that every person has a different reason for pursuing professional education. Some may want to become teachers, while others may be exploring new career opportunities or seeking personal development. Despite our different backgrounds, we share a common experience: we are all making an effort to learn, improve ourselves, and achieve our individual goals.

Through our conversations and group activities, I have learned to appreciate different opinions and perspectives. I realized that learning does not happen only when the instructor discusses a lesson. Sometimes, we learn valuable things from our classmates through their experiences, questions, ideas, and personal insights. Listening to their stories has helped me understand that everyone has their own struggles and motivations, even if we do not always see them.

Our group activities have also taught me the importance of teamwork and cooperation. Working with different people requires patience, understanding, and a willingness to listen. There may be differences in opinions, working styles, or approaches to completing a task, but these situations provide opportunities to develop our interpersonal skills. I learned that successful teamwork is not simply about dividing responsibilities; it is about communicating effectively, respecting each member's contribution, and working toward a common goal.

The friendships I have developed throughout this journey have made the experience more meaningful. Having classmates who share similar academic responsibilities helps create an environment where we can encourage and support one another. When requirements become difficult or schedules become demanding, knowing that other people are experiencing similar challenges reminds me that I am not alone.

I believe that the relationships I have built during PEC will remain a valuable part of my experience. More than the lessons written in our notebooks, the friendships, shared experiences, and memories we create contribute to our personal growth. They remind me that education is not only an individual journey but also a shared experience in which people can learn from and inspire one another.

Learning from Our Instructors and Teachers

Another significant part of my PEC journey has been learning from our instructors. Their knowledge, guidance, teaching approaches, and feedback have helped me understand the responsibilities involved in becoming an educator. As I listen to their discussions and observe how they facilitate learning, I begin to recognize the amount of preparation and dedication required to teach effectively.

Before taking PEC, I understood teaching mainly as the process of explaining lessons and providing students with information. However, my experiences in the course have helped me realize that teaching involves much more than delivering content. Teachers must prepare lessons according to learning objectives, identify appropriate teaching strategies, consider the needs of different learners, and evaluate whether students have understood the lesson. They must also create an environment where students feel respected, encouraged, and comfortable participating in the learning process.

I have also learned the importance of accepting feedback. Sometimes, we may believe that we have completed a task correctly, but the comments and suggestions of our instructors can reveal areas where we need to improve. Rather than viewing corrections as failures, I am learning to treat them as opportunities for growth. This attitude is important because a future teacher must be willing to evaluate personal performance, recognize weaknesses, and make adjustments when necessary.

Our instructors have also helped me appreciate the value of patience and understanding in education. Every student has a different learning pace, level of confidence, and way of understanding information. An effective teacher must be able to recognize these differences and provide appropriate support. This realization encourages me to become more considerate and open-minded as I continue preparing for a possible teaching career.

I have come to understand that teachers are not only sources of knowledge but also role models who can influence students through their actions, attitudes, and values. Their professionalism, fairness, dedication, and willingness to help demonstrate the qualities that future educators should develop. Through their guidance, I am learning to look beyond the academic requirements of PEC and focus on the kind of teacher I hope to become.

The Lessons I Learned About the Teaching Profession

Throughout my PEC journey, I have gained a deeper understanding of the responsibilities and challenges of teaching. One of the most important lessons I learned is that every learner is different. Students may have different abilities, interests, backgrounds, personalities, and learning needs. Because of this, teachers must avoid assuming that one teaching strategy will work equally well for everyone.

This realization has helped me appreciate inclusive education and the importance of creating learning opportunities for all students. A good teacher must recognize that some learners may need additional guidance, alternative explanations, more time, or different learning materials. Rather than focusing only on the students who easily understand the lesson, teachers must also consider those who need extra support. I learned that being an effective educator requires empathy, patience, flexibility, and respect for individual differences.

Another lesson I learned is the importance of classroom management. A classroom is composed of individuals with different behaviors and levels of participation. Teachers must establish clear expectations, encourage positive behavior, maintain a productive learning environment, and respond appropriately when challenges arise. Classroom management should not simply be about controlling students; it should also be about helping them develop discipline, responsibility, respect, and cooperation.

I also gained an appreciation for lesson planning and assessment. Lessons should have clear objectives and appropriate activities that help students achieve the intended learning outcomes. Teachers must carefully choose learning materials, methods, and assessments that are relevant to the lesson and suitable for the learners. Assessment, in particular, should not be viewed only as a way of assigning grades. It should also help teachers identify what students have learned, determine which concepts need further explanation, and improve their teaching strategies.

Furthermore, I learned that effective teaching requires creativity and adaptability. Teachers must be prepared to adjust their methods depending on the situation and the needs of their students. They must also continue learning because educational practices, technologies, and learner needs continue to change. These lessons have helped me understand that becoming a teacher is a continuous process of development rather than an achievement that ends upon obtaining a license.

The Role of Information and Communication Technology in Education

One of the important areas I have come to appreciate during my PEC journey is the use of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) in teaching and learning. Technology has changed the way information is presented, accessed, and understood. Compared with traditional approaches that rely mainly on textbooks, chalkboards, and printed materials, teachers today have more opportunities to make lessons interactive and engaging through digital tools.

Through my learning experiences, I have realized that ICT can help teachers present information through videos, presentations, educational websites, interactive activities, and other digital resources. These tools can make abstract concepts easier to understand and provide students with different ways to engage with the lesson. Technology can also encourage collaboration, creativity, independent learning, and active participation.

However, I have also learned that using technology does not automatically make a lesson effective. Teachers must carefully select tools that support the learning objectives rather than using technology simply because it is available. They must also consider whether students have access to the necessary devices and internet connection, and they should provide alternative learning methods when needed.

Another important realization is the need to promote responsible and ethical technology use. Students must learn how to evaluate online information, protect their privacy, respect intellectual property, and use digital platforms appropriately. As a future educator, I believe that I must also continue developing my own digital skills so that I can guide students in using technology safely and productively.

This lesson is particularly meaningful because it reminds me that teachers must adapt to the changing needs of society. If I become a teacher, I want to use appropriate technology to make learning more accessible, practical, and interesting while maintaining the human connection that is essential to education.

Balancing My Studies, Work, and Personal Responsibilities

One of the challenges of my PEC journey is balancing my academic responsibilities with my current employment and personal life. Being a working student requires discipline because I must manage my time carefully and make sure that I fulfill my responsibilities in both areas. There are times when work demands attention while academic requirements also need to be completed. These situations can become stressful, especially when several tasks need to be accomplished within a limited period.

Through these challenges, I have learned the importance of time management and prioritization. I realized that I cannot always depend on motivation alone. There are days when I feel tired or overwhelmed, but I still need to make responsible decisions about how I use my time. Setting priorities, organizing tasks, and preparing ahead can help me manage my responsibilities more effectively.

I have also learned the importance of discipline and consistency. Small efforts, when done regularly, can contribute significantly to achieving a larger goal. Completing assignments early, reviewing lessons, participating in discussions, and preparing for assessments are habits that can help me become a more responsible student.

Another lesson I learned is that progress does not always happen quickly. There may be moments when I feel uncertain about my abilities or wonder whether I can successfully manage all my responsibilities. During these times, I remind myself why I started this journey. I want to improve my qualifications, explore the teaching profession, and prepare for future opportunities. Remembering these goals helps me continue even when the process becomes challenging.

My experience as a working PEC student has also taught me to value rest, patience, and self-awareness. I need to recognize my limitations and manage my responsibilities in a way that allows me to continue learning without neglecting other important aspects of my life. This experience is helping me develop the perseverance and sense of responsibility that I will also need in my future career.

Discovering My Strengths and Recognizing My Weaknesses

My PEC journey has encouraged me to reflect on my personal strengths and the areas where I still need improvement. One of the strengths I can bring to this course is my willingness to learn. Even after completing my bachelor's degree and entering the workforce, I remain open to new experiences and opportunities for development. I believe that this attitude is important because learning should not stop simply because we have already completed a particular stage of education.

My professional experience has also helped me develop responsibility, teamwork, communication, and adaptability. Working in an institutional environment has taught me the importance of following procedures, coordinating with other people, and completing tasks properly. These experiences can help me adjust to the expectations of the teaching profession, where collaboration, organization, and professionalism are essential.

At the same time, I recognize that there are areas where I need to improve. Returning to an academic environment requires me to strengthen my study habits, improve my confidence in classroom discussions, and continue developing my skills in lesson preparation and instructional delivery. I also need to learn how to explain concepts in ways that are understandable to students with different learning abilities and backgrounds.

Rather than allowing these weaknesses to discourage me, I see them as areas for development. I understand that nobody becomes an effective professional overnight. Improvement requires practice, feedback, reflection, and a willingness to learn from mistakes. By acknowledging my weaknesses, I can identify the steps I need to take to become more competent and confident.

This process has taught me that personal growth begins with self-awareness. I must understand what I can already do well while remaining honest about the skills I still need to develop. As I continue my PEC journey, I want to use every learning opportunity to strengthen my abilities and prepare myself for the responsibilities that may come in the future.

Connecting My Previous Degree to My Future Teaching Career

My background in Hotel and Restaurant Management is an important part of my identity, and I do not consider taking PEC a departure from everything I have learned before. Instead, I see it as an opportunity to connect my existing knowledge with a new professional direction.

Hospitality education involves both theoretical understanding and practical application. Students must learn concepts related to service, communication, management, professionalism, and customer relations, but they must also develop the ability to apply these concepts in actual situations. My background gives me a foundation that I may eventually share with students who are preparing for careers in hospitality, tourism, or related fields.

For example, my experiences have helped me appreciate the importance of teamwork, attention to detail, effective communication, problem-solving, and providing quality service. These competencies can be valuable in helping students understand the expectations of the professional world. If I have the opportunity to teach in a related field, I hope to connect classroom discussions with practical examples and real-world situations.

At the same time, PEC is teaching me that subject knowledge must be supported by sound teaching practices. It is not enough to share personal experiences; I must also know how to organize lessons, explain concepts, assess understanding, and create opportunities for students to practice what they learn. This is where professional education becomes valuable because it helps me understand how to transform knowledge into meaningful learning experiences.

I now see my two educational paths as complementary. My first degree provides me with a foundation in hospitality, while PEC helps me develop the educational knowledge and skills needed to share that foundation with others. Together, they can help me explore opportunities where my previous training and newly acquired teaching competencies can work hand in hand.

My Hopes for a Better and More Meaningful Future

As I continue my PEC journey, I carry hopes that extend beyond completing the course. One of my goals is to fulfill the necessary requirements to pursue a professional teaching license and explore opportunities in the education sector. I want to challenge myself to become someone who can contribute to the development of students and help them prepare for their future careers.

I also hope to improve my professional qualifications so that I can be better prepared for suitable employment opportunities, including positions in government service. My present work at JHCSC Pagadian Annex has given me valuable experience, and I am grateful for the opportunity to contribute to an institution. However, I also recognize the importance of planning for long-term career development. I want to continue improving my skills and preparing myself for opportunities that align with my qualifications and goals.

More than career advancement, I want my journey to have a meaningful purpose. I want to become a person who does not stop learning after achieving one milestone. I want to remain open to new challenges, develop new skills, and use my experiences to help other people. Whether my future leads me toward teaching, continued government service, or another opportunity where my qualifications are useful, I believe that the knowledge and values I gain from PEC will continue to benefit me.

I understand that success is not always immediate, and the path toward my goals may involve uncertainty. There may be applications, examinations, additional requirements, and challenges that I will need to face. Nevertheless, I believe that preparation, perseverance, and continuous learning will help me move forward. I cannot control every opportunity that comes my way, but I can control the effort I put into preparing myself.

Conclusion: A Journey That Continues Beyond the Classroom

Looking back on my journey toward enrolling in the Professional Education Course, I realize that this decision represents more than the pursuit of another qualification. It reflects my willingness to grow, my desire to explore new opportunities, and my determination to prepare for a better future. As a BSRM graduate and a contractual employee at JHCSC Pagadian Annex, I have learned that professional development does not end with our first degree or our first job. Instead, every experience can become a foundation for further growth.

Through PEC, I have gained a deeper understanding of the teaching profession, the responsibilities of educators, and the importance of creating meaningful learning experiences. I have learned about lesson planning, classroom management, assessment, inclusive education, and the appropriate use of technology. These lessons have helped me realize that teaching requires not only knowledge but also patience, empathy, creativity, discipline, and a genuine commitment to helping learners succeed.

Beyond academic knowledge, I have also gained valuable experiences from meeting new friends and classmates, working with different people, and learning from our instructors. These interactions have taught me the importance of teamwork, respect, cooperation, and continuous improvement. They have reminded me that education is not simply about individual achievement but also about the relationships and shared experiences that help us become better individuals.

My journey has also taught me to appreciate the value of perseverance. Balancing work, studies, and personal responsibilities can be challenging, but these experiences are helping me become more disciplined and determined. I have learned that pursuing a dream requires patience and consistent effort, and that even small steps can bring us closer to our goals.

As I move forward, I hope to complete my professional education requirements, pursue my goal of becoming a licensed teacher, and explore opportunities that will allow me to continue growing professionally. I know that the road ahead may not always be easy, but I am willing to learn from every challenge and make the most of every opportunity.

Ultimately, my PEC journey has reminded me that it is never too late to begin again, discover a new passion, or work toward a different dream. My first degree, my current employment, and my experiences as a PEC student are all important parts of my journey. They have shaped my perspective and helped me understand the kind of professional and individual I want to become.

I began this journey with the hope of opening new career opportunities, but I am discovering that its value goes far beyond employment. It is teaching me to become more understanding, responsible, confident, and willing to learn. As I continue, I will carry the lessons, friendships, and experiences I have gained, knowing that they will help guide me toward my future.

For me, the Professional Education Course is not the end of one journey but the beginning of another. It is an investment in myself, an opportunity to serve others in a new way, and a reminder that growth begins when we have the courage to take the next step. I may not know exactly where this path will lead me, but I am determined to continue learning, improving, and working toward a future where I can use my knowledge and experiences to make a positive difference in the lives of others.

This reflection is a living document — a snapshot of where I am now, and a reminder of the growth I still hope to achieve.
