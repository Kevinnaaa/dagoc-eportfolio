About Me

I graduated with a Bachelor of Arts in Hotel and Restaurant Management, and I am currently working at JH Cerilles State College, Pagadian Annex.

As a person, I consider myself hardworking, responsible, adaptable, and willing to learn new things. One of my strengths is that I can adjust to different situations and work with different kinds of people. I am also committed to the tasks assigned to me and always try my best to complete them properly.

Learning is a continuous process — and I want to discover what I can contribute to the field of education. — Brendan Jadrian W. Dagoc

However, I also have weaknesses. Sometimes, I tend to overthink things because I want to make sure that my work is done correctly. I also recognize that there are areas where I still need to improve, especially in my confidence when speaking in front of a group. Nevertheless, I see these weaknesses as opportunities to learn, grow, and become a better version of myself.

I decided to take Professional Education as my second course because I want to explore new opportunities, broaden my knowledge, and develop skills in teaching and education. Although my first degree is in Hotel and Restaurant Management, I believe that learning is a continuous process, and I want to discover what I can contribute to the field of education.

Through this journey, I hope to develop my teaching abilities, gain more confidence, and become someone who can share knowledge and make a positive impact on other people's lives.

That is all about me. Thank you, and have a great day!
